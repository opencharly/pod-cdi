# 2026.329.2115 — feat(pod-cdi)!: pin the require-free layer-cdi — a pod that names nothing is toolkit-free

## Summary

Pin `layer-cdi` to `v2026.329.2058` — the contract release whose `require: layer-cuda` is gone
(opencharly/layer-cdi#24). The toolkit now installs driver-free at the source, and the consumer
**class** decides who composes it; this base becomes toolkit-free.

The pin's comment is rewritten: it used to explain why the *require* mattered (the old tag pinned the
release that pulled `layer-cuda`); it now explains the opposite — a pod that names nothing is
toolkit-free, and a class-2/3 consumer composes `layer-cuda` itself
(opencharly/opencharly#1287).
